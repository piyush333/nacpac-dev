# NACPAC — Brand & Branding Brief
**Version 1.0 · May 2026**

---

## 1. What NACPAC Is

NACPAC is an in-house UV printing studio based in Delhi, India, selling short-run custom stickers and packaging accessories directly to small D2C brand founders — online, with upfront payment, and no sales rep in the middle.

The entire business is built on one insight: the Indian packaging market requires 500+ piece minimums on custom print jobs. That locks out every founder who is testing a new product, launching a home brand, or simply doesn't have warehouse space. NACPAC removes that lock. The minimum order is **25 pieces**.

## 3. Target Audience

**Primary:** First-time and early-stage D2C founders in India — specifically:
- Soap and skincare makers
- Candle pourers
- Food brands (home bakers, artisan condiments, snacks)
- Stationery and merch creators
- Anyone launching a brand who needs to look credible without committing to a 500-piece run

**Psychographic:** 18–35 years old. Found on Instagram. Built their brand on Canva. They know what they want their product to look like — they just couldn't afford to prove it until now. They are not packaging buyers; they are founders who happen to need packaging.

**The key insight for all copy:** they have never bought custom print before. Industry terms like MOQ, MOV, bleed, cut-line, UV coat mean nothing to them. Every customer-facing word must pass the test: *"Would a candle-maker understand this in 3 seconds without Googling anything?"*

## 5. Voice & Tone

**Voice:** Fun and proving a point. NACPAC speaks like a founder who got tired of being told "sorry, minimum 500" and built the thing they wished existed. Warm, direct, a little rebellious — but never condescending.

**Tone on the website:** Conversational. Short sentences. Numbers tell the story, not terms.
**Tone in product copy:** Practical and reassuring. The customer is making a decision about their brand's appearance — respect that.
**Tone in error states / ops communication:** Clear and human. Never bureaucratic.

**Rules:**
- No acronyms in customer-facing copy. Ever. (MOQ, MOV, GSM, etc.)
- Strike out numbers, not terms: ~~500-piece minimums~~ → 25 stickers from ₹399
- Frame the minimum as a low bar, not "no minimum" (which is inaccurate and undermines trust)
- "From 25 pieces" > "25 piece minimum" > "no minimums"
- The rebellion is in the number, not the vocabulary

## 7. Mascot

A **tape roll character** — yellow body, expressive eyes, small feet. The mascot is the brand's friendly face. It lives as a floating widget on the website (bottom-right corner), reacts to mouse proximity, and delivers contextual messages as the user scrolls through sections.

**Personality:** Curious, helpful, a bit playful. Not a sales rep. More like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know everything about stickers.

**Mascot rules:**
- Never overwhelming — it is a supporting character, not the main event
- Gestures (wave, bounce, point, spin) fire once per page load — it says hello, it doesn't follow you around shouting
- Bubble messages are written in the same voice as the page copy — no jargon, no pressure

## 8. Visual Identity

### 8a. Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Indigo** | `#29266c` | Primary — text, borders, backgrounds, CTAs |
| **Purple** | `#5a3493` | Secondary — accents, highlights, eyebrow text |
| **Purple Soft** | `#7e58b8` | Tertiary — hover states, subtle accent |
| **Lavender** | `#dccee6` | Light accent — eyebrow backgrounds, borders |
| **Lavender Soft** | `#ece4f2` | Subtle backgrounds — section fills, cards |
| **Gold** | `#f5b11b` | Accent — button shadows, strikethrough bars |
| **Yellow** | `#fdd223` | Highlight — nav CTA, price pills, mascot body |
| **Yellow Pale** | `#fee17a` | Soft accent — hero gradient, subtle fills |
| **Cream** | `#fff8e6` | Section background — how-it-works |
| **Paper** | `#fdfbf6` | Base page background — warm off-white |
| **Ink** | `#1a1830` | Primary body text |
| **Mid** | `#56516a` | Secondary text — subtext, captions |
| **Line** | `#e8e2d6` | Borders, dividers |

**Color use principles:**
- Indigo is the anchor. Every section and component should have indigo as a structural element.
- Yellow is the energy. Used on CTAs, price points, mascot — anything that needs immediate attention.
- Lavender/Paper/Cream are breathing room. Sections alternate between these to give the eye a rest.
- Never use yellow for body text. It is always a background or highlight color.

### 8b. Typography

| Role | Font | Weights | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headings** | Fredoka | 400, 500, 600, 700 | Rounded, friendly, distinctive |
| **Body** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 | Clean, highly legible at small sizes |
| **Handwritten accent** | Caveat | 600 | Used sparingly — sticker face text, informal labels |

**Type scale:**
- Eyebrow labels: `0.78rem`, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.16em`
- Section sub-copy: `1.1rem`, `line-height: 1.6`
- Section headings (h2): `clamp(2rem, 4.4vw, 3.2rem)`, Fredoka 600
- Hero heading (h1): `clamp(2.6rem, 6.2vw, 5rem)`, Fredoka 600
- Body text: `1rem`, `line-height: 1.55`
- Fine print / labels: `0.85rem`

### 8c. Shape Language

- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped (border-radius: 999px). Curved shapes drive higher CTR vs. sharp angles (backed by eye-tracking research).
- **Cards:** Rounded corners (20–48px radius depending on card type). Always with `border: 2px solid var(--indigo)` and `box-shadow: Npx Npx 0 var(--indigo)` — the flat shadow creates depth without skeuomorphism.
- **Sticker mock-ups:** Slightly rotated, overlapping — references real sticker packs.
- **Eyebrow labels:** Pill-shaped with lavender background. All-caps, 0.78rem.

### 8d. CTA Design Principles

- **Primary CTA:** Indigo background, paper text, gold flat shadow (`box-shadow: 6px 6px 0 var(--gold)`). Hover: translate -2px,-2px, shadow expands to 8px.
- **Secondary/Ghost CTA:** Transparent background, indigo text, indigo flat shadow (`box-shadow: 3px 3px 0 var(--indigo)`). Hover: lavender fill, shadow expands.
- Both CTAs must have at least one depth cue (shadow or fill) — ghost buttons with no depth cue have the worst conversion performance.
- Nav CTA: Yellow background, indigo text and border — highest contrast combination in the palette.

- **Location:** Delhi, India

- **Contact:** hello@nacpac.in

## 11. What NACPAC Is Not

- Not a bulk print house. Not for orders of 5,000 stickers at ₹2/pc.
- Not a design agency. Artwork intake = upload or hire-an-hour; not full brand identity work.
- Not a marketplace. One studio, one press, one quality standard.
- Not for enterprises. The minimum exists (25 pieces). The audience is founders, not procurement teams.
