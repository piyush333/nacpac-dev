How it works

File to front door,
four steps.

01

Send your brief

Drop artwork, dimensions and quantity via WhatsApp or email. We confirm specs and price within hours.

02

We send a soft proof with cut-line preview. Nothing goes to press until you sign off.

03

Print & cut

Job hits the press the moment you approve. Cut, laminate and pack-out happen same day.

04

Dispatched

Tracked courier across India. You get a link the moment it leaves the studio.
