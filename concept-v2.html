04 — Questions

Things people ask.

Everything you want to know before you upload. Still stuck? Tapey and the team reply on WhatsApp within an hour.

What's the minimum order quantity?

50 pieces. We're built for small businesses — no 1,000-unit minimums, no "sorry we only print in bulk." Order what you need, reorder when you need more.

How long from order to delivery?

Proof within 24 hours. Once approved, printing takes 2–3 business days and shipping takes 2–4 days across India. Most orders land at your doorstep in under a week.

What file formats do you accept?

PDF, AI, EPS, PNG, JPG. For die-cut stickers, a vector file (PDF/AI) is ideal. Don't have print-ready art? Our in-house designer prepares it from ₹499.

Are the stickers waterproof?

Vinyl, transparent and holographic stickers are fully waterproof and UV-resistant. Paper labels are splash-resistant but not fully waterproof — best for indoor packaging.

Do you provide GST invoice?

Yes. Every order comes with a GST-compliant tax invoice. Claim input credit on your business purchases.

What if I don't like the proof?

Free revisions until you're happy. We only print once you sign off — no surprises, no wasted stickers.
